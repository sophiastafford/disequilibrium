## What Changed This Week

Who gets paid when AI does professional work? This week, three institutions gave an answer. In law, medicine and consulting, the money is starting to follow the AI, so the safest first job is the one that builds it, checks it, or can overrule it. Latham & Watkins, a large corporate law firm, set aside $1 million to reward associates who build AI tools. Medicare’s new roadmap would pay for parts of a checkup delivered by AI. KPMG’s consulting chief said some advice clients once paid for no longer exists, because they now get it from AI themselves. Each of those moves takes away a paid task and creates a paid role for the person who makes the tool, verifies it, or has the authority to say no. The broader market is still slow. Goldman Sachs expects a “low-hire, low-fire” economy for another year. The rest of this issue follows where the money is moving in three fields. As you compare offers, ask which of those three roles the job puts you in.

## Law

**Latham is putting $1 million behind associates who build AI tools, and HSF Kramer just hired six legal engineers.** Latham’s new Associate AI Impact Award pays associates for AI tools, workflows and training they create, with payouts tiered by impact. The firm is also reworking how it trains junior lawyers because new hires arrive already fluent in AI. Separately, Herbert Smith Freehills Kramer hired six legal engineers from Norm.AI, a legal AI company, in what Artificial Lawyer calls the first major move of AI-native talent into Big Law. If you’re applying to firms, prepare one concrete example of a task you improved with AI, and treat “legal engineer” as a real track alongside the associate path. ([Above the Law](https://abovethelaw.com/2026/11/biglaws-latest-ai-incentive-for-associates-comes-with-a-1-million-prize-pot/) | [The American Lawyer](https://www.law.com/americanlawyer/2026/11/12/lathams-ai-fluent-junior-associates-prompt-firm-to-revamp-training-playbook/) | [Artificial Lawyer](https://www.artificiallawyer.com/2026/11/16/hsf-kramer-hires-6-legal-engineers-from-norm-ai/))

**About 4,800 lawyers and staff at two UK firms are getting legal AI tools, while two-thirds of pre-law students say AI is fit only for brainstorming.** Addleshaw Goddard is rolling out Harvey to more than 3,400 people, and Fieldfisher is giving Legora to 1,400. In a survey of students at 37 colleges, 66.7% of pre-law students limited acceptable AI use to brainstorming, against 38% of students overall. For pre-law students, that skepticism is worth keeping, but put it into checking AI output carefully, since trainees use these tools from day one. ([Legal Cheek](https://www.legalcheek.com/2026/11/addleshaws-and-fieldfisher-pick-rival-legal-ai-giants-harvey-and-legora-as-adoption-race-heats-up/) | [Above the Law](https://abovethelaw.com/2026/11/the-next-generation-of-law-students-dont-trust-ai-hype/))

## Healthcare

**The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) released a roadmap that would let Medicare pay for “AI-led” care, and Google launched an AI agent for clinical paperwork.** The roadmap proposes payment pathways for AI-delivered care, including parts of the Medicare Annual Wellness Visit, and a Food and Drug Administration draft requiring chatbots to pass doctor-like competency tests. The same week, Google’s Gemini for healthcare began targeting chart prep and prior-authorization packets at systems including HCA Healthcare and Northwell Health. Google cites clinicians spending nearly 28 hours a week on administrative work. For pre-meds planning a scribe or authorization-coordinator gap year, this is the work being automated first, so learn electronic health records and payer rules well enough to check the AI’s draft. ([Nextgov](https://www.nextgov.com/artificial-intelligence/2026/11/trump-administration-releases-roadmap-boost-clinical-ai/417254/) | [Becker's](https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/healthcare-information-technology/ai/cms-super-intelligence-roadmap-eyes-paying-for-ai-led-care-7-notes/) | [Google Cloud](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/ai-machine-learning/gemini-for-healthcare))

**Nurses at Sutter Health are bargaining for the right to overrule AI in a contract covering about 10,000 workers.** The California Nurses Association picketed at 13 Sutter facilities, demanding that nurses can override AI-driven decisions and won’t be punished for errors made by unapproved AI tools. Sutter already uses AI for patient notes and chart summaries. At Arizona State, nursing students now share the simulation lab with an AI nurse assistant that is taking the same courses and exams. For nursing students, override rights are now a contract term, so ask employers whether the contract protects your clinical judgment over AI tools. ([Press Democrat](https://www.pressdemocrat.com/2026/11/10/sutter-health-nurses-picket-over-ai-staffing-and-wages/) | [Lake County News](https://www.lakeconews.com/en/85692-sutter-lakeside-nurses-picket-for-staffing-immigration-and-ai-protections-in-new-contract) | [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/drive-health-and-arizona-state-university-advance-the-future-of-safe-clinical-ai-through-the-nurse-assist-initiative-302934626.html))

## Finance & Consulting

**KPMG’s global head of consulting said that part of its advisory business is “literally nonexistent anymore” because clients now do it with AI.** At EY, AI now handles the first round of audit preparation that junior auditors used to do. KPMG’s consulting head argued that firms should keep hiring graduates but give them responsibility earlier. EY is creating new entry roles such as data delivery professionals and product managers. For Big Four and consulting applicants, data, product and tech-risk skills now match the roles these firms are naming. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/12/ai-consulting-billable-hours-graduate-hiring.html) | [Appic News](https://www.appicnews.com/ai-is-coming-for-consultings-billable-hours-and-its-graduate-pipeline/))

**Anthropic launched an AI that takes meeting notes and builds spreadsheets, and Providence already uses AI for accounting entries.** Claude Meetings joins video calls, drafts plans and posts action items, and Claude Sheets builds multi-tab spreadsheets with formulas. Providence, a large hospital system, says AI now handles its accounting journal entries and has removed 1.3 million inbox messages. Meeting minutes, trackers and journal entries have long been first-year analyst work. For finance and consulting interns, the value is moving to the person who catches the error in the AI’s summary or spreadsheet, so practice that before your first day. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/10/anthropic-launches-claude-meetings-to-turn-workplace-discussions-into-plans-and-tasks/) | [Becker's](https://beckershospitalreview.com/finance/were-seeing-it-now-providence-ceo-on-the-ai-productivity-boom))
