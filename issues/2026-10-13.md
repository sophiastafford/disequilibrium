## What Changed This Week

Ropes & Gray, a large law firm, now allows associates to count up to 100 hours spent experimenting with AI toward their annual billable-hour targets. The same week, a Goldman Sachs executive said that new hires will manage AI agents from the start of their careers. Employers now expect new hires to supervise AI from day one, so the jobs worth taking are the ones that still teach you how to judge the work.

That judgment used to come from routine work. Junior analysts and coders learned what good work looks like by reconciling accounts, checking loan documents and writing basic code. Those are the tasks that bank tech hubs in India are hiring fewer people to do, and they help explain why developer postings are recovering mostly for experienced engineers. The other half of the data is the opening. Some employers are paying for juniors to learn the new skill, and Goldman is putting them in charge of AI rather than below it. The rest of this issue covers where the junior job is being redesigned.

## Finance & Consulting

**Goldman Sachs’ Asia-Pacific president said on October 8 that new hires will manage AI agents from the start of their careers.** Speaking at the Milken Institute Asia Summit in Singapore, Kevin Sneader said that juniors have to “make the most of this virtual army,” and he called reassigning middle managers a “generational challenge.” Goldman has already rolled out Devin, an AI coding agent made by Cognition, across a technology division of about 12,000 engineers. The redesign skips the years of routine tasks that used to teach analysts what good work looks like. If you are recruiting for banking, ask how a firm trains analysts to check AI output, because that judgment is now the job. ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-08/young-bankers-to-manage-ai-agents-from-start-goldman-exec-says) | [AI Street](https://www.ai-street.co/p/goldman-puts-new-hires-in-charge))

**Wall Street banks’ tech hubs in India are hiring fewer graduates as AI takes over the tasks that used to start careers there.** Bloomberg reports that banks including JPMorgan and Goldman Sachs are automating loan-document checks, account reconciliation, basic coding and invoicing in India’s Global Capability Centers, which employ about 2.4 million people. Citigroup net-hired about 2,000 people in India this year, down from about 4,000 last year. ING, the Dutch bank, says that the change shows up as slower hiring and new skill requirements rather than mass layoffs. For students eyeing banking operations or tech roles, the squeeze arrives as fewer openings, so skills in AI, cloud or cybersecurity count for more than the generalist route. ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-10-07/ai-crowds-out-fresh-grads-at-wall-street-tech-hubs-in-india) | [China Daily HK](https://www.chinadailyhk.com/article/640738))

## Tech

**U.S. software developer job postings are up about 15% since February 2025, but experienced and AI-focused roles account for most of the gain.** Indeed Hiring Lab, the research arm of the job site Indeed, found the rise even as overall postings fell 7%, and developer postings are still 27.5% below their February 2020 level. Experienced roles made up 71% of the increase. Meanwhile, employment among developers aged 22 to 25 is down about 20% from 2024. For CS majors, the recovery is real but mostly closed to beginners, so roles that pair coding with AI tools are a better target than generic junior postings. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/10/11/ai-software-developer-jobs.html) | [Digital Today](https://www.digitaltoday.co.kr/en/view/112933/ai-coding-tools-us-software-developer-job-postings-rise-15-percent))

**Apple seriously considered replacing about 5,000 AppleCare support workers with AI around July, according to Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman.** Gurman reported on his podcast that the plan is “on ice for now,” and Apple has not announced it. The company has already cut dozens of hardware engineering program managers. Support and coordination roles are a common way into large companies, and they are also the easiest work to point AI tools at. If you are weighing a support or operations job as a way in, look for one with a clear path toward engineering or decision-making work. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/apple-weighed-replacing-5-000-192814134.html))

## Law

**Ropes & Gray now lets more than 1,000 associates and counsel count up to 100 hours of AI training and testing toward their billable targets.** The policy expands an earlier program in which first-year associates could put up to 20% of their billable hours toward AI, and the firm says that associates logged more than 35,000 hours of AI time this year. Akerman, another firm, gives billable credit for qualifying AI projects. This is the counterpart to Goldman’s announcement. Some employers are paying for juniors to build the judgment that supervising AI requires. For law students comparing firms, a billable-hour credit for AI is a concrete sign that a firm will train you rather than expect you to arrive knowing. ([ABA Journal](https://www.abajournal.com/news/article/ropes-gray-allows-more-attorneys-to-count-ai-testing-toward-billable-hours) | [JD Journal](https://www.jdjournal.com/2026/10/12/law-firms-ai-training-billable-hours/))
